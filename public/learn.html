Cyber-Security Basics

A short guide to staying safe online

🔐 What is cyber security?

Cyber security means protecting your phone, laptop, accounts and personal information from people who want to steal, damage or misuse them. It is not only about software. It is also about the way each of us behaves online.

🎣 Common online threats

  • Phishing: fake emails, messages or websites that pretend to be a trusted person or organisation to collect your details.
  • Malware: harmful software, such as viruses and ransomware, that damages a device or locks and steals files.
  • Social engineering: tricking people, by phone or message, instead of breaking into systems.
  • Online and payment frauds: fake offers, lottery messages, QR codes and urgent money requests.
  • Account takeover: hackers log in to your account using a leaked or guessed password.

🔑 Passwords and logins

  • Use long passwords that are hard to guess. A mix of words, numbers and symbols works well.
  • Use a different password for every important account.
  • Turn on two-factor authentication (2FA) wherever it is offered.
  • Never tell anyone your password, OTP, PIN or CVV. Real banks and companies never ask for them.

📱 Apps and devices

  • Install apps only from the official app store and read the permissions they ask for.
  • Keep your phone, laptop and apps updated, as updates fix security problems.
  • Lock your screen and use antivirus protection on your computer.
  • Do not plug unknown USB drives or devices into your computer.

🌐 Safe browsing and Wi-Fi

  • Check the website address carefully before you type any details.
  • Be careful with public Wi-Fi and avoid banking or payments on it.
  • Do not click unknown links or open attachments you did not expect.
  • A padlock in the address bar means the connection is protected. It does not prove the site is trustworthy.

💳 Safe digital payments

  • Scanning a QR code or entering a UPI PIN is only needed to pay money, never to receive it.
  • Confirm any urgent money request from a friend or relative by calling them.
  • Check the name and amount on the screen before you approve any payment.

💾 Protect your data

  • Take regular backups of important files.
  • Think before you share personal details, photos or locations on social media.
  • Remember that deleted files are not always gone for good. Use proper tools for sensitive data.

🚨 If something goes wrong

  • Change your passwords at once and turn on 2FA.
  • Tell your teacher, parents or the college IT department.
  • In India, report cyber-crime on cybercrime.gov.in or call the helpline 1930 (for financial fraud report immediately).
