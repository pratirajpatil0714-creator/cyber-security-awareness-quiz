About the program

An awareness program organised by the Department of Information Technology to help students recognise online threats such as fake messages, OTP frauds and stolen accounts, and to build safe digital habits.

📅 Program dates Three days
🏛️ Organised by Dept. of Information Technology
📍 Venue Government Polytechnic, Kolhapur
